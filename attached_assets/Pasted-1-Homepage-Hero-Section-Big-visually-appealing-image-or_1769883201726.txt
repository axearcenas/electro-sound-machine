1. Homepage

Hero Section:

Big, visually appealing image or video of your equipment at an event.

Headline example: “Professional Sound & Lighting Rentals for Every Event”

Subheadline example: “From intimate parties to large concerts, we deliver high-quality audio and lighting solutions.”

Call-to-action (CTA): “Get a Quote”, “Check Our Packages”

Quick Intro/About Us (2–3 sentences)

Example: “We provide top-notch sound systems and lighting equipment for weddings, corporate events, concerts, and private parties. With years of experience, we ensure your event looks and sounds spectacular.”

2. Services

Break this into sections for clarity:

a. Sound System Rentals

PA systems, speakers, mixers, microphones

DJ equipment, live band setups

b. Lighting Rentals

Stage lights, LED uplighting, moving head lights

Effects: lasers, strobes, fog machines

c. Event Packages / Custom Solutions

Example: “We can create custom packages to suit your venue size, audience, and event type.”

Include photos or videos for each service so clients see what they’re renting.

3. Portfolio / Gallery

Photos of past events

Before & after lighting setups

Short captions: “Wedding reception at XYZ Hall – sound & lighting setup by us.”

This builds credibility and trust.

4. Pricing / Packages

Optional: show starting prices or package examples

Example:

Basic: $200 – sound system + 2 lights

Standard: $500 – full sound system + stage lighting

Premium: $1,000 – complete audio & lighting setup for large events

If you prefer, just include a “Request a Quote” button instead of showing prices publicly.

5. Testimonials / Reviews

Real client feedback

Example: “Lynuel Sound & Lights made our wedding unforgettable. Highly recommended!”

6. About Us

Your story, experience, and mission

Example: “Founded in 2015, we’ve been powering events of all sizes with quality sound and lighting. Our mission: make every event unforgettable.”

7. Contact / Booking

Phone number, email, physical address (if any)

Booking form: Name, Event Date, Event Type, Services Needed

Optional: Google Maps for location

8. Extras (Optional but recommended)

FAQ: “Do you deliver & set up?” / “Can I rent for 1 day only?”

Blog / Tips: Event planning advice, “How to choose the right sound system”

Social media links / Instagram gallery for recent events
